Privacy policy

Your books stay yours. This page explains how BookTrace handles information when you use the app.

Contact support

Your library and iCloud

Books, reading progress, sessions, categories, ratings, notes, quotes and goals are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, BookTrace synchronizes this information through your private iCloud database using your Apple Account. BookTrace does not create a separate account for you or operate a server containing your library. iCloud storage is provided by Apple.

Book searches and covers

Online searches send the words you enter, ISBNs, authors or subject queries to Open Library and, when needed, Google Books. Importing books may send ISBN lookups to these catalog services. Cover images are downloaded from the image URLs supplied by the catalogs. These providers receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address, and handle requests under their own policies. Recommendation scores are calculated on your device; fetching candidate books may send subject queries, rather than your complete reading history, to a catalog.

Camera and text recognition

Camera access is requested when you scan a barcode or a page. Page text recognition runs on your device. BookTrace does not upload page photographs to an OCR service. You can edit recognized text before saving it as a quote. Saved quote text and notes become part of your library and may synchronize through iCloud.

Purchases

Apple processes payments for BookTrace Pro. BookTrace receives verified purchase information, such as the product and its entitlement or expiration status, to unlock features. A local purchase-status cache helps preserve access when offline. BookTrace does not receive your payment card details.

Exports and sharing

You decide when to export a backup or share an image. Exported CSV and JSON files can contain personal reading information. JSON backups include reading sessions, quotes, notes and goals. When you choose a destination in the system sharing interface, the selected app or service receives the exported content.

Advertising and tracking

This release does not include advertising, tracking SDKs or a developer-operated analytics service. It does not request access to your contacts or precise location.

Retention and your choices

Your library stays on your device and, when synchronized, in your iCloud account until you remove it. Deleting a book removes its related sessions and quotes. Library erasure removes saved library content; with iCloud enabled, deletions may synchronize to your other devices. Exported copies and device or iCloud backups are managed separately by you or Apple. Camera permission and iCloud access can be changed in iPhone Settings.

Contact and support messages

BookTrace is developed by Semih TAKILAN. If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send remain in our support mailbox to handle your request and follow-up. Contact the same address to ask about or request deletion of support correspondence. We do not need your Apple Account password or payment card information.
