BookTrace support

A little help for your next chapter

Questions about your library, a purchase or a reading session? We are here to help.

Contact support

Start your library

Find a book in Explore, open it and add it to your library. Search and book discovery need an internet connection; saved books and reading sessions remain available offline. There is no book limit and no BookTrace account to create.

BookTrace Pro

Your library, basic journal, iCloud sync, local recommendations and Goodreads import are free. Pro adds Live Activity, widgets, reading goals, detailed statistics, the quote notebook, exports and the full year in review. The summary of your reading year is free to share.

Restore a purchase

Open BookTrace Pro from the app’s settings and select Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account used for the original purchase and an internet connection. Apple manages subscription billing. A lifetime purchase is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

iCloud is not syncing

Check the sync status in BookTrace settings. Confirm both iPhones use the same iCloud account, iCloud is available and there is sufficient iCloud storage. Open BookTrace on both devices with a connection and allow time for changes to arrive. The app can continue locally if iCloud is unavailable. Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app while investigating missing data.

Scanning a quote

In the quote notebook, scan a page or type the quote manually. Scan a flat, well-lit page, then review and edit the recognized text. Recognition takes place on your iPhone and may make mistakes, especially with diacritics or unusual fonts. You can change camera access in iPhone Settings.

Import or export your library

Use the library import screen to select a Goodreads CSV or BookTrace JSON backup and review it before importing. Import is free. With Pro, export CSV for compatible catalog tools or JSON to retain sessions, notes, quotes and goals. Keep a separate copy of important backups.

Get in touch

Email booktrace.help@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, BookTrace version and the steps that led to the issue. Share screenshots only if you are comfortable with their contents. Never send an Apple Account password or payment card number.
